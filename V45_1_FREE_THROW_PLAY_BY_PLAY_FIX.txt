Scoridium V45.1 — Free throws in Play-by-Play

Fixes a tracker Play-by-Play regression: Quick Track associates each free throw
with its source foul via linkedEventId. The Play-by-Play filter previously hid
all linked events, including both made and missed free throws. Each FT attempt
now appears in Play-by-Play and uses the existing player/team, quarter, make/
miss and running-score display. Other linked event handling is unchanged.

To install: replace files from this ZIP in your Scoridium repository and push
main. No new Supabase SQL is required. Run a test shooting foul with two free
throws (one made, one missed); check both tracker Play-by-Play and View Game.
